Where your hands go
  • Rest your fingers on the normal home row: left hand on A S D F, right hand on J K L ;. Those are the bottom steno row. Reach up one row for the top steno row.
  • Thumbs do the vowels. Left thumb sits between C and V (A and O). Right thumb sits between N and M (E and U).
  • Each finger owns one column. When a stroke needs both keys in a column, like T and K, press the gap between the two keys with that one finger.
  • A stroke is a chord. Press every key in it, in any order, then let go of all of them. The stroke is read when the last key comes up.
  • Left S is on both Q and A. The star is on T G Y H. Use whichever is free.
  • Mac keyboards sometimes drop a key when many are held at once. If a stroke keeps coming out short, switch on “One key at a time”: tap the keys one after another, then press Space to send the stroke. Esc clears it.
  • If Plover is running, turn its output off while you practice here, or it will swallow the keys.
